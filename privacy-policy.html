1. Who we are

EazyDezk is a billing and customer relationship management product developed and operated by Tovance Private Limited ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains how we handle personal information in connection with our website, product, demos, and support. For privacy questions or requests, email contact@tovance.com.

2. Information we process

Depending on how you use EazyDezk, information may include your name, business name, email address, phone number, account details, and communications with us; business records you or your team submit, such as customer and supplier contacts, invoices, tax identifiers, transactions, inventory, expenses, and CRM notes; and technical information such as IP address, browser, device information, and service activity recorded when you access the service. Please provide only information needed for your business use and avoid unnecessary sensitive personal information.

3. Why we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, arrange demos, create and administer accounts, provide billing and CRM features, provide support, communicate service updates, troubleshoot problems, protect the service against misuse, and meet applicable legal and accounting obligations. Where consent is required, we will seek it for the relevant purpose. You may opt out of promotional messages by contacting us; essential account and service messages may still be sent.

4. Business records and your customers

Your business determines what customer, employee, supplier, and other personal information it adds to its workspace. You are responsible for having the authority and providing the notices and permissions required to use that information. We process workspace information to provide the service on your business’s instructions, subject to applicable law and any separate agreement. If your information was added by an EazyDezk customer, please contact that business first; we can help route requests where appropriate.

5. Face Data and Employee Selfie Information

Collection of selfie images

EazyDezk collects employee photographs (selfies) through the device camera when employees use attendance check-in or field-visit verification features. These photographs are submitted voluntarily through the application’s attendance functionality for employment-related verification purposes.

Purpose of collection and use

The photographs are collected exclusively to verify employee attendance and check-in activities; verify employee field visits; allow authorized company administrators and managers to manually review and confirm employee attendance; and support internal human resource management and attendance records.

No facial recognition or biometric identification

EazyDezk does not use facial recognition technology, facial geometry extraction, biometric identification, or facial matching algorithms to identify employees. Photographs are reviewed manually by authorized managers and administrators. We do not create or maintain biometric face templates or facial recognition profiles.

Storage and security

Employee selfie photographs are securely uploaded to and stored on Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure. Access is restricted to authorized personnel who require the information for attendance verification and related administrative purposes. We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect these photographs against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse.

Sharing and disclosure

Selfie photographs are accessible only to authorized personnel within the employee’s organization for legitimate attendance and HR verification purposes. We use AWS as a third-party infrastructure and storage service provider to host and securely process this information on our behalf. AWS is required to maintain appropriate security and confidentiality protections for the information it processes. We do not sell, license, or share employee selfie photographs with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Data retention and deletion

Employee selfie photographs are retained for a maximum period of 30 days from the date of collection. After this period, the photographs are automatically deleted from our active storage systems in accordance with our retention procedures.

Consent and withdrawal

For employee selfie photographs collected through attendance and field-visit verification, employees may request access, deletion, or withdrawal of consent by contacting their organization’s administrator or emailing contact@tovance.com. We will process valid requests in accordance with applicable laws and our data retention obligations.

Third-party data protection

Any third-party service provider involved in storing or processing employee selfie photographs is required to maintain appropriate confidentiality, security, and data protection safeguards consistent with the protections described in this Privacy Policy.

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information processed, but no service can guarantee absolute security. Service providers may process information in locations where they operate, subject to applicable transfer requirements. Contact us if your business needs specific hosting or data-processing terms.

9. Your choices and requests

You can contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or raise a privacy concern.
