1. Who we are

EazyDezk is a billing and customer relationship management product developed and operated by Tovance Private Limited ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains how we handle personal information in connection with our website, product, demos, and support. For privacy questions or requests, email contact@tovance.com.

2. Information we process

Depending on how you use EazyDezk, information may include your name, business name, email address, phone number, account details, and communications with us; business records you or your team submit, such as customer and supplier contacts, invoices, tax identifiers, transactions, inventory, expenses, and CRM notes; and technical information such as IP address, browser, device information, and service activity recorded when you access the service. Please provide only information needed for your business use and avoid unnecessary sensitive personal information.

3. Why we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, arrange demos, create and administer accounts, provide billing and CRM features, provide support, communicate service updates, troubleshoot problems, protect the service against misuse, and meet applicable legal and accounting obligations. Where consent is required, we will seek it for the relevant purpose. You may opt out of promotional messages by contacting us; essential account and service messages may still be sent.

4. Business records and your customers

Your business determines what customer, employee, supplier, and other personal information it adds to its workspace. You are responsible for having the authority and providing the notices and permissions required to use that information. We process workspace information to provide the service on your business’s instructions, subject to applicable law and any separate agreement. If your information was added by an EazyDezk customer, please contact that business first; we can help route requests where appropriate.

5. When information is shared

Information may be shared with service providers that help operate, host, maintain, or support EazyDezk, to the extent needed for their work; with people your business authorizes to access its workspace; or with an integration you choose to enable. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect legal rights or service security, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or business transfer subject to applicable safeguards. We do not sell personal information.

6. Cookies and third-party services

The service may use cookies or similar storage for functions such as sessions and preferences. You can manage cookies through your browser, although disabling them may affect functionality. If optional analytics or advertising technologies requiring consent are introduced, we will provide the relevant information and choices. Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which involves requests from your browser to Google and may disclose your IP address and browser information. Third-party websites and integrations have their own privacy policies.

7. Storage, retention, and security

We retain information for the purposes described in this policy and for applicable legal, accounting, security, and dispute-resolution needs. Retention depends on the information and the purpose; deletion from backups may occur later in the normal backup cycle. We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information processed, but no service can guarantee absolute security. Service providers may process information in locations where they operate, subject to applicable transfer requirements. Contact us if your business needs specific hosting or data-processing terms.

8. Your choices and requests

You can contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or raise a privacy concern. Available rights and exceptions depend on applicable law and our role in processing the information. We may need to verify your identity and authority before acting. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing, and some records may need to be retained by law. Please use the subject “EazyDezk Privacy Request” when writing to contact@tovance.com. We will handle requests and grievances within the timelines required by applicable law.

9. Children

EazyDezk is intended for business use by adults and is not directed to children under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate authorization, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as our services or legal obligations change. The updated date will appear on this page, and we will provide additional notice of material changes where required. Privacy enquiries and grievances should be addressed to Tovance Private Limited at contact@tovance.com.
