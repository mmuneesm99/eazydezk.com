How can we help?

Contact us for help with your EazyDezk account, billing and CRM features, or a technical issue.

Contact support

Email our team at contact@tovance.com.

EazyDezk is developed and operated by Tovance Private Limited. Use the subject “EazyDezk Support Request” so we can identify your enquiry.

Help us understand the issue

When you contact us, please include:

  • Your business name and the email address associated with your account.
  • A description of what you were trying to do and what happened.
  • Your device model, operating system, and EazyDezk app version, if available. For website issues, include your browser.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue and any error message or screenshot that may help.

Please do not send passwords, one-time codes, payment card details, or unnecessary customer information. Hide sensitive details in screenshots before sharing them.

Account and subscription help

For sign-in problems, account access, billing questions, or cancellation requests, email us with a short description of your request. We may need to verify your identity or authority before making account changes.

Privacy and data requests

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to ask about closing your account, contact contact@tovance.com with the subject “EazyDezk Privacy Request”. See our Privacy Policy for details about how we handle information and requests.
